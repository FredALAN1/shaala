# SHAALA (شعلة) — Milestone 1 (Vertical Slice) Task Breakdown

**Milestone Goal:** Deliver a playable, self-contained, verified Godot 4.3 vertical slice spanning **Desert Camp**, **Empty Highway**, and **Empty Riyadh** (6 interconnected rooms + loop shortcut), featuring tight platforming (Run, Jump, Coyote Time, Jump Buffer, Variable Jump, Dash, Wall-Cling), 3-tier Resource-scaled Flame Dagger combat, Flame Spell, Parry stagger, 3 distinct enemies, 1 multi-phase boss (*The Fractured Spire Warden*), 2 Memory Orbs with vignettes, 1 Orb Shrine with atomic save/load, 3 Torn Notebook Pages, Arabic-first RTL UI with live English switching, and automated QA verification.

## 3. Shared Cross-Worker Contracts (Quick Reference)

- **Autoload Order (`project.godot`):** `EventBus` → `GameState` → `SettingsManager` → `SaveManager` → `AudioManager`.
- **Physics Layer Bitmasks:** `world=1`, `one_way=2`, `player=4`, `enemy=8`, `player_hitbox=16`, `enemy_hitbox=32`, `interactable=64`, `hazard=128`.
- **M1 Ability IDs:** `flame_spell` (start), `dash` (`orb_desert_dash`), `wall_cling` (`orb_highway_wall_cling`).
- **M1 Room IDs:** `desert_camp_01`, `desert_camp_02`, `empty_highway_01`, `empty_highway_02`, `empty_riyadh_01`, `empty_riyadh_boss`.
- **M1 Shrine & Shortcut IDs:** `shrine_desert_camp` (in `desert_camp_01`), `shortcut_riyadh_to_camp` (`empty_riyadh_01` → `desert_camp_01` spawn `from_riyadh_shortcut`).
- **M1 Notebook Page IDs:** `page_00_camp`, `page_01_highway`, `page_02_mall`.
- **M1 Boss ID & Key:** `boss_spire_warden` (`BOSS_SPIRE_WARDEN_NAME`).
- **Spawn Safety Rule:** Every `Spawns/<id>` `Marker2D` sits `~34 px` above a solid floor inside `camera_bounds` and at least `90 px` clear of any `TransitionGate` trigger.

## 4. M1 Definition of Done (DoD)

Milestone 1 is **DONE** when all of the following conditions hold simultaneously:
1. **Structural & Resource Integrity:** `project.godot` and every `.tscn` / `.tres` / `.gd` / `.json` / `.gdshader` file parse cleanly with zero missing `res://` references, zero broken sub-resources, and matching autoload/signal/layer contracts.
2. **Playable End-to-End Vertical Slice:** Farid can start in `desert_camp_01`, speak with The Boy and Amira, rest/save at `shrine_desert_camp`, acquire **Dash** and clear the 380 px ravine, meet Motaib on the highway, acquire **Wall-Cling** and climb the 220×480 px pylon chimney, open `shortcut_riyadh_to_camp` back to camp, defeat **The Fractured Spire Warden**, collect all 3 M1 Torn Notebook Pages, and reach the sealed M2 Metro gate.
3. **Zero Softlocks & Zero Dead Ends:** Automated geometry simulation confirms Dash and Wall-Cling gates block sequence breaks without their ability, allow smooth completion with their ability, never trap the player in pits or transition ping-pong loops, and maintain a valid return path from every room.
4. **Arabic-First RTL & BiDi Excellence:** Game launches in Modern Standard Arabic (`"ar"`) with proper RTL layout, HarfBuzz Arabic shaping, safe BiDi numeric/keycap rendering, zero hardcoded user-facing strings, and instant live switching to English (`"en"`).
5. **Persistence & Accessibility:** Saving at the Orb Shrine, collecting orbs/pages, opening shortcuts, and defeating the boss persist across reloads (`user://saves/slot_0.json`), and all accessibility/control settings persist in `user://settings.json`.
6. **QA Sign-Off:** `python3 tools/verify_m1.py` exits clean with **0 critical and 0 high defects**, and `docs/QA_NOTES.md` + `docs/KNOWN_ISSUES.md` are published by `qa-verifier`.
